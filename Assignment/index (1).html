01 / KNOW YOUR PIECES

Six pieces.
Six possibilities.

Every piece has its own way of moving.
Meet the team before making your first move.

♚
THE ONE TO PROTECT

The king

Moves one square in any direction, but never onto an attacked square. Checkmate means the king is attacked and there is no legal escape. Castling is a special king-and-rook move with additional conditions.

AIM / Keep your king safe
♛
THE VERSATILE ONE

The queen

Moves any number of squares horizontally, vertically or diagonally. Like a rook or bishop, she cannot jump over other pieces. A clear board gives her room to work.

MOVEMENT / Straight lines + diagonals
♜
THE STRAIGHT SHOOTER

The rook

Moves any number of squares along a rank or file: horizontally or vertically. It cannot jump over pieces. Open files give a rook useful lines of activity.

MOVEMENT / Horizontal + vertical
♝
THE DIAGONAL THINKER

The bishop

Moves any number of squares diagonally without jumping over pieces. Each bishop stays on the same colour of square throughout the game.

MOVEMENT / Diagonals only
♞
THE UNEXPECTED ONE

The knight

Moves in an L: two squares in one direction, then one at a right angle. It is the only piece that can jump over other pieces. Knights often work well near the centre.

MOVEMENT / Two across, one over
♟
THE SMALL BUT MIGHTY ONE

The pawn

Moves forward one square, or two on its first move if both squares are empty. It captures one square diagonally forward. Reaching the far rank promotes it to a queen, rook, bishop or knight. En passant is a special, time-limited pawn capture.

MOVEMENT / Forward to move; diagonally to capture

02 / YOUR FIRST FEW MOVES

Think simply.
Play thoughtfully.

You don’t need to memorise a hundred openings. Start with three useful ideas.

  1. 01

    Make room in the centre.

    Central pawns and pieces can influence more of the board. Try developing around e4, d4, e5 and d5.

  2. 02

    Bring your pieces into play.

    Develop knights and bishops instead of moving the same piece again and again without a reason.

  3. 03

    Give your king a safe home.

    Prepare to castle when legal. King safety matters more than a tempting, but risky, early attack.
